The Industry Antidote.

For too long, the landscaping industry has been built on a foundation of exploitation. Companies often treat employees as expendable overhead and the land as a product to be "forced" into compliance with chemicals.

Filthy Rich Soil Co. is different. We believe that the person tending the soil is as important as the soil itself. We provide a professional environment where safety is a non-negotiable standard and your expertise in environmental science is cultivated daily.

Living Wage Guarantee

We believe in a landscape industry where professionals can support families. Our pay scales reflect the specialized skill of soil stewardship, starting well above industry averages.

Safety-First Culture

Your health is our priority. We provide comprehensive health benefits, fitness club discounts, and healthy life incentives. Daily stretching breaks are built into the workday because we believe in protecting your body for the long term.

Career Growth & Training

We hire at the entry level and invest in your development. Through our transitional phase program, you'll learn soil science, arborist standards, and native plant management—building real expertise that moves you up in the company.

Profit-Sharing & Bonuses

Your work directly contributes to our success, and you deserve to share in it. We offer profit-sharing to all team members and substantial bonuses to top earners who consistently demonstrate excellence and leadership.

Ready to Change the Industry?

We hire at the entry level and develop you into a professional. If you're passionate about environmental stewardship and eager to learn soil health, native plant management, and professional craftsmanship, we want to hear from you.
